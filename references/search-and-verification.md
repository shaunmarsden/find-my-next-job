# Search and verification

1. Turn the candidate brief into supported role families and hard constraints. Ask only for missing facts that can change exclusion decisions. Do not equate a desired job title with demonstrated experience.
2. Search one or two primary role families and an adjacent duty-based family. Include geography/working pattern. Use more than one source route; save queries and failed access as well as successes. Stop when additional queries repeat the same sources or no eligible results remain; explain the boundary rather than claiming market coverage.
3. Prefer the employer's careers page or an ATS page linked to that employer. Open it in the actual browsing environment. Record the role, posting/requisition ID, URL, time with timezone, and observed presence/closure. An accessible job description and application route are presence evidence, not proof the employer is actively processing applicants. Never submit or test submission. A cached snippet is only a discovery lead.
4. Read the description and relevant public eligibility text. Separate essentials from preferences; degree-or-equivalent differs from degree-only. Record each essential criterion with candidate evidence and `supported`, `partial`, `gap` or `unknown`. Preserve alternatives such as a named reporting tool or similar experience, and separately check any additional requirement for basic knowledge of the named tool. Do not turn transferable operational duties into SQL, regulated compliance, engineering or professional licences.
5. Check location, office frequency, eligible remote countries, weekly hours, shifts, contract duration and pay. Preserve missing values. For a hard actual-salary floor, an undisclosed figure or unresolved pro-rata basis is `unknown`; a range maximum alone does not guarantee the floor. If the stated pro-rata terms establish that even the maximum actual pay is below the hard floor, mark the pay constraint unmet and exclude on those terms. Keep source verification status separate: an unverified repost can still advertise incompatible terms. Do not invent full-time reference hours to calculate an exact amount. Do not infer annual pay from an hourly rate unless contracted/paid hours and the relevant basis are explicit. No guessed exchange rates or benefit valuations.
6. If the current official page contradicts a search snippet, use the official page and retain the discrepancy. If official fields themselves conflict, hold the role and identify the exact unresolved question. Do not silently pick the convenient interpretation.
7. Follow a repost's public outbound link only to identify the original vacancy. Record the shared ATS/employer posting ID. Use one recommendation for one job. Keep possible same-title duplicates for review if identity is unresolved.
8. Do not bypass access restrictions, logins, CAPTCHAs or rate limits. Do not bulk scrape. Public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby board endpoints are possible company-specific sources, not global search engines. Public technical access does not grant unrestricted resale rights. This package integrates no live API; review current provider/employer terms before any integration.

## Manual review target

A second human should check every proposed shortlist's original links and the most consequential fit/exclusion claims. Check at least one rejected near-match too. Record who reviewed, date, disagreements and disposition. Do not invent a human sign-off or use an AI confidence percentage as a substitute. Unsupported conclusions stay pending.
