# First interaction and shortlist example

Observed in one fresh Codex test on 28 October 2026. The customer is fictional; the linked vacancies were real public pages inspected in that session. This is a shortened presentation of the saved response, not a promise that these jobs remain open. No suitable lead was fully cleared.

**Customer:**

Use Job Match Finder to find up to three jobs I could realistically consider. I'm a fictional person called Rowan for this test. I've worked for four years coordinating a small homewares warehouse in Manchester: planning rotas for six people, answering customer emails about orders, updating stock spreadsheets with Excel formulas, and writing handover checklists. I haven't worked in software, freight forwarding or regulated finance. I don't have a degree, SQL skills or formal professional qualifications. I want full-time work, either in Manchester or fully remote within the UK. I can work in the UK. I'd like about £30,000 but it isn't a firm minimum. I don't know which job titles to search for. Please keep it simple and tell me what I should check next.

**Agent:**

Your strongest starting points are warehouse administration, order coordination and customer service. I haven't found a fully cleared match in this search. These opportunities need checks first:

- **[Striqr: Customer Service](https://striqr.com/careers/customer-service): conditional.** UK remote, full-time; £25,000 to £30,000 advertised, pay period unstated. Customer emails and checklists are relevant. Confirm the account/billing troubleshooting and support-tool experience needed at entry.
- **[FULFIL.X: Customer Service / Account Manager](https://fulfilx.co.uk/careers/customer-service): conditional.** Order enquiries and warehouse coordination overlap. Confirm CRM requirements, occasional on-camera work and the exact site.
- **[FULFIL.X: Fulfilment Operative](https://fulfilx.co.uk/careers/fulfilment-operative): conditional.** Warehouse experience is relevant. Check site, pay, shifts and whether hands-on picking/packing suits you.

Three other original descriptions could not be read, so they are not application recommendations. Would you prefer customer support or hands-on warehouse work? I can show the detailed source and requirement checks if you want.

The agent began without an intake questionnaire because the request already contained enough search information. It inferred role families rather than asking for target titles. The customer was not asked to create technical files or run commands. The complete, unabridged response is preserved with the test evidence outside the buyer package.
